What Are Plantation Shutters?

Plantation shutters are interior window coverings made with wide louvers that can be adjusted to control light and airflow. They are typically crafted from materials such as wood, vinyl, or composite. Their classic design suits both modern and traditional homes, making them a versatile option for any room.

Types of Plantation Shutters Available

Plantation shutters come in several types, each designed to meet different needs. Wooden shutters are known for their natural beauty and durability, making them ideal for living rooms and bedrooms. Vinyl shutters are moisture-resistant and work well in kitchens and bathrooms. Composite shutters offer a balance between affordability and durability, making them a popular all-around choice.

You can also choose between full-height shutters, tier-on-tier designs, or café-style shutters depending on how much coverage and flexibility you want for your windows.
